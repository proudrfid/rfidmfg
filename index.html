About RFID MFG

Three decades of precision RFID manufacturing

Founded in 1996, RFID MFG Co., Ltd. has focused on RFID and smart-card production. We design, research, produce and deliver hotel keycards, proximity & contact-IC cards, RFID labels and inlays, magnetic-stripe cards, PVC ID cards and the readers that bring them to life.

Our Shenzhen production base runs card lamination, inlay bonding, die-cutting and personalisation in house, backed by a self-developed full-process traceability and QC system — so every order ships with consistent quality, whatever the volume.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What does RFID MFG manufacture?

We are a direct RFID and smart-card manufacturer producing RFID & NFC cards, key fobs, wristbands, labels & inlays, RFID tags, RFID-blocking products and readers/IoT hardware — all customizable to your chip, size, artwork and encoding.

Are you a manufacturer or a trading company?

A manufacturer. RFID MFG has produced RFID and smart-card products since 1996 from its own plant in Shenzhen, where card lamination, inlay bonding, die-cutting and personalisation all run in house. The site holds ISO 9001, ISO 14001 and ISO 45001 certification. Buying direct means factory pricing and full control over customization and quality.

What is your minimum order quantity (MOQ)?

MOQ is flexible and depends on the product — often from a few hundred pieces for cards, wristbands and key fobs, and from around a thousand for labels and inlays. Share your target quantity for an exact wholesale quote.

Can I get a free sample before ordering?

Yes. Free pre-production samples are available so you can verify material, print and chip performance before committing to a full run.

Do you offer custom OEM / ODM and chip encoding?

Yes — full OEM/ODM with in-house R&D: custom chip, frequency, size, material, artwork, packaging and pre-encoding, with custom keys under NDA.

How long does production take?

Typically 3–7 days for samples and 7–20 days for bulk production depending on product family, after artwork and encoding are approved, then worldwide shipping. We reply to quotes within 24 hours.
