How to order custom RFID products from a China factory

By RFID MFG Editorial Team · Updated August 13, 2026 · 2 min read

In short: Six steps: send your spec, get a written quote within 24 hours, test free samples in 3–7 days, approve the artwork proof, bulk production runs 7–30 days (by product family) with 100% read testing, and tracked door-to-door delivery follows. Payment is by T/T and everything carries a 2-year warranty.

Step 1 — Define your spec (or just describe the goal)

The five things that define an RFID order: chip or frequency (match your existing readers — this is the most common mistake), size and form factor, material, artwork, and quantity. If you can only answer some of these, describe the application instead — "we need 5,000 wristbands for a 3-day festival with tap payment" is enough for our engineers to spec the rest.

Step 2 — Written quote within 24 hours

You get an exact, binding price for the stated spec — not an indicative range. If you are still comparing options, the price guide on this site shows the real range for every product before you ever send an email.

Step 3 — Free samples in 3–7 days

Pre-production samples cost nothing and exist so you can test the actual chip, material and print on your own readers and in your own environment before committing. If a sample fails your tests, we adjust chip, antenna or material and resample — bulk never starts on a failed sample.

Step 4 — Artwork proof and approval

Alongside samples you receive an artwork proof for print layout, colours and variable data. Encoding files, sector maps and keys are handled under NDA. Production starts only after you approve both sample and proof — this approval gate is your protection.

Step 5 — Bulk production with QC (7–30 days by product family)

Lamination, inlay bonding, die-cutting and personalisation run in house, with full-process traceability and a 100% read test before packing. Typical bulk lead time is 7–30 days depending on product family, construction and volume; the exact date is on your quotation.

Step 6 — Delivery and after-sales

Orders ship express, door to door, with tracking and full export documentation. Every product carries a 2-year warranty with ongoing technical support — if something reads wrong in the field, you talk to the engineers who built it.

Common mistakes to avoid

Ordering a frequency your readers cannot see (always confirm reader model first); skipping the sample stage to save a week and losing a production run; sending encoding data after production has started; and leaving artwork sign-off to the last minute — the proof cycle is fast, but only if someone on your side owns it.

Frequently asked questions

What payment terms do you accept?

T/T (bank transfer). Exact terms and any deposit schedule are stated on the written quotation before you commit.

Can you encode our existing system keys and sectors?

Yes — custom keys, sector maps and serialisation are handled under NDA, so replacement or expansion orders drop straight into your current system.

What artwork files do you need?

Vector files (AI, PDF or EPS) print best. Send what you have — you approve a proof before anything is printed, so file issues are caught before production.

What happens if the samples fail our tests?

We adjust the chip, antenna or material and send new samples. Bulk production only ever starts on a sample you have approved.
