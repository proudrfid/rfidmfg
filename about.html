RFID MFG Profile

Who we are

RFID MFG Co., Ltd. designs, researches, manufactures and sells a full range of RFID and smart-card products.

Our line-up spans RFID hotel keycards, proximity and contact-IC chip cards, RFID labels and inlays, magnetic-stripe cards, PVC ID cards, RFID wristbands, key fobs and the matching readers/writers. We work closely with both overseas and domestic chip suppliers to guarantee a steady supply of first-hand chips — keeping quality high and pricing competitive. New to RFID? Start with our complete RFID cards guide or the LF vs HF vs UHF frequency guide.

RFID MFG products serve hotels and hospitality, access control, identification, education, warehouse and logistics, retail and apparel, healthcare, events and livestock, and are exported to the USA, Canada, Europe and across Asia. See real deployments in our case studies. We offer OEM/ODM services, R&D and technical support, on-time delivery and a 2-year warranty on everything we make.

FAQ

About RFID MFG — frequently asked questions

Where is RFID MFG located?

RFID MFG Co., Ltd. is headquartered in Shenzhen, Guangdong, China, where it operates its self-built production base. We ship worldwide.

How long has RFID MFG been manufacturing RFID products?

Since 1996 — nearly three decades. The company began focusing on RFID smart cards in 1999, brought its first production line online in 2001, and moved into its current technology park in 2013.

What certifications does RFID MFG hold?

We are certified to ISO 9001, ISO 14001 and ISO 45001, and our products meet CE, FCC, FSC, RoHS and REACH requirements.

What is your production capacity?

Card lamination, inlay bonding, die-cutting and personalisation all run in house at our Shenzhen site, with chips sourced directly from NXP, Impinj and EM Microelectronic product lines. Exact capacity depends on the construction — share your annual volume and we will confirm lead times.

Do you offer OEM / ODM and custom manufacturing?

Yes. We provide full OEM and ODM services with in-house R&D — custom chip, size, material, artwork, encoding and packaging, with custom keys and encoding available under NDA.

What is the minimum order quantity, and can I get a sample?

MOQ is flexible and depends on the product and configuration, and free pre-production samples are available so you can verify material, print and chip before a full run. Request a quote with your target quantity.
