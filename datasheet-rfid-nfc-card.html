RFID / NFC Card — Datasheet

13.56 MHz & UHF cards for access, transit and tap-to-share.

Contactless RFID/NFC cards for access control, transit, loyalty and tap-to-share digital experiences. One frequency band per card — LF, HF/NFC and UHF are not interchangeable; we help you match the band and chip to your readers.

Specifications

Technical details

Frequency bandsLF 125 kHz · HF/NFC 13.56 MHz · UHF 860–960 MHz — one band per card; bands are not interchangeable
HF / NFC chips (ISO/IEC 14443 Type A)MIFARE Classic® EV1, MIFARE DESFire® EV2/EV3, NTAG213/215/216 (NFC Forum Type 2)
HF vicinity chips (ISO/IEC 15693)ICODE® SLIX
LF chips (125 kHz)EM4200; T5577 (programmable, EM/HID-compatible formats)
UHF chips (EPC Class 1 Gen 2 / ISO/IEC 18000-63)NXP UCODE®, Impinj Monza series
MaterialPVC
SizeCR80
Read rangeTap ≈ up to 10 cm (HF); up to several metres (UHF) — reader- and environment-dependent

Operating frequency for this product family: LF 125 kHz · HF / NFC 13.56 MHz · UHF 860–960 MHz.

All specifications are customizable. RF read performance depends on reader, antenna, tag size and mounting environment — values above are typical for standard builds; confirmed specifications are stated on the written quotation and approved sample.
