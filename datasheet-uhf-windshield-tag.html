UHF Windshield Tag — Datasheet

Long-range tags for vehicle access and parking.

UHF windshield tags mount inside a vehicle windscreen for long-range identification at parking barriers, gated communities and toll lanes — reading vehicles at speed from several metres. Tamper-evident versions void on removal.
